Hollow Blocks

Three nights in a block world that doesn't want you in it. Dig, build, and keep your eyes on them.

  • Touch: left side drags to walk, right side drags to look. Tap to hit a zombie or break a block. Hold to place one.
  • Keyboard: WASD, mouse look, click to break or attack, right-click to place, Space to jump, 1–4 to pick a block.
  • They move faster when you aren't looking at them. Wall yourself in before dusk.

On iPhone: rotate to landscape, and use Share → Add to Home Screen for a full-screen build without the address bar.
